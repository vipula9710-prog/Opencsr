# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful and constructive, accept feedback gracefully, focus on what is best for the community, and show empathy towards others.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the project maintainers via the repository's private contact channels. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be respected. Maintainers may remove contributions or temporarily or permanently ban contributors who violate this code.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1, including enforcement guidelines, applies and is available at the link above.
